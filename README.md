### 🪞 Overview

TraitKeeper is a next-generation NFT marketplace and valuation engine redefining digital ownership.
Instead of static collectibles, NFTs become living digital identities that evolve through traits, behavior, and perception.

At its core lies NFT Vitality — a dynamic valuation model that measures NFTs by performance, influence, and health rather than floor prices.

TraitKeeper is the foundation of a larger ecosystem of interconnected layers:

EchoSafe — Immutable Certification of Ownership

Outerflow — Community-as-a-Service Network

Parallel Lines — The Architecture of Perception

1. NFT Vitality System

A next-generation valuation engine replacing floor price with NFT Vitality, based on:

Encrypted dashboard for managing NFTs, viewing private bids, and controlling trade visibility.
